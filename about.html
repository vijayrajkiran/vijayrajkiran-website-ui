About

Vijay Rajkiran

I'm a product manager who ended up in AI by way of geospatial data. My M.Sc. in GIS at UCL started me on questions about how to turn large, messy spatial datasets into decisions — and that same question (how do you turn data and models into something a product can rely on) is what's carried through every role since: building a B2B SaaS analytics platform in Singapore, governing ML and Gen AI data at a global media company, scaling an enterprise AI platform processing aerial imagery, and now leading Gen AI product work in EdTech.

What I bring to a team isn't a specific model or stack — it's judgment about when AI is the right answer, how to evaluate whether it's working, and how to ship it responsibly. That's as true advising a team for a few weeks as it is owning a roadmap full-time.
