How I Work

A point of view on shipping AI products

The judgment calls I bring to every AI product engagement — whether advising a team for a few weeks or owning the roadmap full-time.

Evaluate before you scale

Every model or Gen AI feature gets a baseline and an eval metric before it reaches users — not after.

Build vs. buy, honestly

Most teams over-build. I push for the smallest AI investment that proves the use case, then scale what works.

Responsible AI isn't a checklist

Governance, accessibility and compliance get designed into the roadmap, not bolted on before launch.

Data platform first

AI products are only as good as the pipeline feeding them. I start there, not with the model.
